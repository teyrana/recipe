# Fajitas


## Ingredients:Seasoning:

- 1/22 cup minced onion
- 2 T chili powder
- 1 T salt
- 4 t cornstarch
- 1 T dried minced garlic
- 1 T ground cumin
- 1 T crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 t beef bouillon granules
- 2 t dried oregano

## Ingredients: Protein
- 1 lb Protein (eg Chicken)
- 1/2 cup water

## Directions:

1. Combine the seasoning ingredients. 
2. In a skillet, brown beef; drain.
3. Add 2 T taco seasoning, and 1/2 C water per lb of protein.
4. Bring to a boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes.
