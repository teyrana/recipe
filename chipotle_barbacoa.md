# Chipotle Barbacoa

This Chipotle Barbacoa is full of intense flavors such as garlic, cayenne, and sweet, smoky chipotle. Make sure you dial back the cayenne and serrano if you can't handle the heat because this recipe is packin' lots of it!
Serve on corn or flour tortillas garnished with cilantro and topped with lime cilantro rice (from this website), black beans, pico de gallo, guacamole, cheese, lettuce, and sour cream.


- Prep Time: 25 mins
- Cook Time: 6 hrs 5 mins
- Total Time: 6 hrs 30 mins
- Servings: 6


## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 lb beef chuck roast, trimmed and cut into 1" chunks
- 4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 1/4 cup apple cider
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 6-12 cl garlic, peeled
- 4 t cumin
- [1 serrano chile pepper, chopped]
- [1 T Ancho Chili Powder]
- [1 T ground cayenne pepper]
- [Omitted -- try with?] 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- [Omitted -- try with?] 1 t ground black pepper
- 1 t salt
- [optional] ½ t ground cloves or to taste
- 1 C chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 bay leaves

## Directions

1. Heat oil in a large skillet over low heat, gradually increasing heat to medium-high. Cook beef in hot oil until browned, about 10 seconds per side. 
2. Transfer beef to a slow cooker.
3. Combine chipotle peppers, apple cider, lime juice, garlic, cumin, serrano pepper, cayenne pepper, oregano, black pepper, garlic powder, salt, and cloves in a blender or food processor; blend until smooth. Pour over beef in the slow cooker.
4. Stir broth, onion, and bay leaves into the slow cooker.
5. Cook on Low until beef shreds easily with a fork, 6 to 8 hours. Remove beef from slow cooker and shred beef using 2 forks. Return shredded beef to the slow cooker and stir to combine with sauce before serving.
