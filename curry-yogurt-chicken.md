# Curry Yogurt Chicken

## Ingredients:
- ?? lb Two chicken breast
- 3 C Yogurt
- 3 t Curry powder
- 1 t Salt
- 1/2 t White pepper
- 1/2 t Cinnamon powder
- 1 C Lime juice

## Methods:
1. Smack the chicken breast to break down the hard fiber tissue to make it easier to absorb the ingredients, then cut into chicken finger style
2. Combine yogurt, curry powder, salt, white pepper, cinnamon, and lime juice.
3. Marinate for at least 2 hours, overnight will be better.
